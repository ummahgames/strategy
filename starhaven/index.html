FIELD NOTES / 001

A keeper’s guide to the stars

01 / Nurture

Click empty space to drop food near hungry creatures. Amoebas and polyps eat star-matter. Adult amoebas shed solar motes for jellyfish. Polyps make plankton for whales. Rays eat minerals; leviathans eat crystals.

02 / Gather

Click glowing treasures before they drift away. Hold and sweep to gather several, or use Collect all / C. Leave some minerals for rays and crystals for leviathans.

03 / Protect

Hold on an invader to fire your orbital cannon. Asteroids cause impact damage; pirates steal credits; parasites drain creatures; leeches damage the hull. Upgrade your cannon and repair the hull in the shop.

04 / Explore

Buy three beacon components to finish a mission. New sectors unlock species, and mission completions unlock companions. The final Ark also requires defeating the flagship. Progress saves locally; export a backup from Flight deck.

Keyboard & accessibility

Focus the biodome, use arrows to aim, and Enter or Space to feed, collect, or fire. C collects all; F feeds the hungriest basic-food creature; X targets the nearest threat; P pauses. Buttons also work with Tab and Enter. Inspection pauses flight. Reduced motion is available in the flight deck. All modes pause when you switch tabs.

Other expeditions

Time Run: build income for five minutes. Escalation: beacon prices increase over time. Sanctuary: a persistent, peaceful habitat with no enemies or starvation. Nothing happens while you are away.
