# Saffron Vanguard artwork

The battlefield uses original code-drawn canvas illustrations and procedural WebGL geometry. The game includes no portrait assets. All runtime files and artwork are local; there are no remote asset requests.

`caravan-camp.png` is an original camp illustration generated with the built-in image generation tool. The same image and notes are copied into the 3D edition by `sync-3d.mjs`.

## Final camp image prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide scenic banner for a fantasy tactical RPG's between-battle caravan camp. Original lush hand-painted landscape illustration, cinematic sophisticated game art, smooth painterly rendering with beautiful light, absolutely no pixel art, voxels or block shapes. Fictional trade-route world inspired by Middle East, Africa and South Asia: a small tranquil caravan camp beneath tall date palms and flat-crowned acacia trees, elegant saffron and indigo striped canvas awnings, richly woven terracotta and teal carpets, brass lanterns, ceramic water jars, rolled blankets, a low glowing fire; distant warm sandstone caravanserai arches and blue layered mountains under a star-filled twilight sky. A few tiny calm travelers in layered cloth sit around the fire, landscape dominates. Eye-level wide composition with breathing room and rich depth, warm amber light against deep indigo and turquoise dusk. No text, no letters, no logos, no religious symbols. Horizontal panorama.
