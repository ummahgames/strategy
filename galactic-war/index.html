How to play

Guided mode introduces mechanics gradually: begin with ordinary planets and capture neutral territory before attacking the enemy. Planet type symbols are shown in the command center legend. Custom mode enables all mechanics immediately. Blue is your faction; red is the opponent. The four difficulty modes change AI timing and planning. Victory unlocks the next numbered level; retry keeps the same layout. There is no final level.

Capture neutral and enemy planets to grow your production. Fleets spend time traveling, so defenders may grow before arrival.

Select a blue planet, then a destination. In route mode, orders create persistent arrows that send ships above a 10-ship reserve. In hybrid mode, tap for a one-time fleet or drag for a route. Click friendly planets to switch sources. Click the selected planet, click empty space, or press Escape to deselect. For friendly reinforcements, drag to the destination or use Choose reinforcement target. Select a source and clear its routes to stop the flow.

Batch production completes five ships at once. Hyperlanes restrict orders to connected planets. Spend ships on production upgrades, but leave enough to defend.
