COMMAND CENTER

Select a planet

Select one of your planets, then select another planet to send a fleet.

Drag between planets or tap a source and destination. Scroll to zoom. Space pauses.

CREATE A BATTLE

Command the galaxy

Choose how your planets build and deploy their fleets.

In hybrid mode, tap to send a fleet; drag to create a route. Routes retain 10 ships for defense.

How to play

Capture neutral and enemy planets to grow your production. Fleets spend time traveling, so defenders may grow before arrival.

Select a blue planet, then a destination. In route mode, orders create persistent arrows that send ships above a 10-ship reserve. In hybrid mode, tap for a one-time fleet or drag for a route. Select a source and clear its routes to stop the flow.

Batch production completes five ships at once. Hyperlanes restrict orders to connected planets. Spend ships on production upgrades, but leave enough to defend.
